Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

typefaster is a free typing test. This page explains in plain language what happens with your information when you use it.

What the typing test collects

Nothing. There are no accounts and no sign-up. The words you type, your speed (WPM) and your accuracy are worked out inside your own browser and are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Technical data

Like every website, the company that hosts typefaster may automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the visit. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics tool to count visits and see which pages and languages are popular. It is used only to improve the site.

Advertising

typefaster may show ads to cover its costs. If it does, advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them. You can learn how Google uses data and manage ad personalization at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and myadcenter.google.com. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Turkey), ads that use cookies will only be shown with your consent.

Cookies

The typing test itself does not set cookies. Any cookies come from the services described above (analytics or advertising), and you can block or delete them in your browser settings.

Children

typefaster is meant for general audiences, including students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information through the typing test, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the address below.
